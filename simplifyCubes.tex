Simplify
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:simplifyCubes:20}
S = \frac{\lr{\cube{4} - 1}\sqrt{\cube{9} + \cube{16} + \cube{96}}}{\cube{6} - \cube{4} - \cube{3} + 2}
\end{equation}

Let
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:simplifyCubes:40}
\begin{aligned}
a &= \cube{2} \\
b &= \cube{3},
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
and note
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:simplifyCubes:60}
\begin{aligned}
4 &= 2^2,\quad 6 = 2 \cdot 3, \\
9 &= 3^2, \quad 16 = 2^4 \\
96 &= 2^5 \cdot 3,
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
so
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:simplifyCubes:80}
S = \frac{ \lr{ a^2 - 1 } \sqrt{ b^2 + a^4 + a^5 b }}{a b - a^2 - b + a^3 }.
\end{equation}

Some trial and error shows that \( a - 1 \) is a divisor of the denominator
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:simplifyCubes:100}
a b - a^2 - b + a^3 = \lr{ a^2 + b }\lr{ a - 1 },
\end{equation}
so
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:simplifyCubes:120}
S = \frac{ \lr{ a + 1 }\sqrt{ b^2 + a^4 + a^5 b }}{a^2 + b }.
\end{equation}

Now square everything
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:simplifyCubes:140}
\begin{aligned}
S^2
&= \frac{ \lr{a + 1 }^2 \lr{ b^2 + a^4 + a^5 b } }{ a^4 + 2 a^2 b + b^2 } \\
&= \frac{ \lr{a + 1 }^2 \lr{ b^2 + 2 a + 2 a^2 b } }{ 2 a + 2 a^2 b + b^2 } \\
&= \lr{a + 1 }^2.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

We've found
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:simplifyCubes:160}
S = a + 1 = \cube{2} + 1.
\end{equation}
